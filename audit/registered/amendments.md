## Amendment 1 (1 Nov 2026, before any price download)

Made after a pre-download review. No price data has been received and no result of any kind has been examined. The registered text above is unchanged and its SHA-256 is unchanged. The four entry definitions, thresholds, research and validation periods, costs, seed, sample sizes and decision rules (a), (c) and (d) are not altered.

| # | What changed | Why | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **CHDN is excluded from the pool before the draw**, not removed afterwards. The seed (20261031) and sizes (250, 250, 120) are unchanged. CHDN is still downloaded as the case study and labelled `case_study`. The script also stops with an error if an excluded symbol ever reaches sample.csv | The registered wording ("removed if drawn") would have left a population smaller than registered and the code did not enforce it | A test fails if CHDN appears in sample.csv |
| 2 | **Benchmarks are judged by role.** ^GSPC is mandatory with no fallback. Russell 2500 is satisfied by ^R25I or SMMD. Russell 3000 Growth by ^RAG or IWZ. The replay uses ApexScan's own rule at each date: the primary if it has more than 50 usable bars, otherwise the fallback if that has more than 50, otherwise the role is unavailable. Nothing else is ever substituted (SPY will not stand in for ^GSPC). The series actually used is written on every replay row and in the data-quality report. When ^GSPC is unavailable the day's relative strength is marked not evaluable, not set to 0.0. The live scanner's silent 0.0 can be reproduced only on request, labelled, for Test B diagnostics | The first validator required all five symbols and would have flagged a normal fallback as a failure. The first replay treated a missing benchmark as 0.0 and passed the gate | ApexScan's optional Massive.com fallback for ^GSPC needs an API key and is not available in the replay. This is a stated limitation |
| 3 | **Holm correction.** The three validation-period comparisons (E2, E3, E4 against E1) test the difference in mean net 63-session return. Method: bootstrap resampling tickers as clusters (10,000 resamples, seed 1), two-sided percentile p-value. Both unadjusted and Holm-adjusted p-values are reported. Decision rule criterion (b) now reads: Holm-adjusted p below 0.05 with a positive difference. Unadjusted 95% intervals are still shown. No approach is ranked or named a winner on observed return | A single registered interval did not control for three comparisons | Criterion (b) is stricter |
| 4 | **Missing data.** Sessions come from the ^GSPC calendar, never from the stock's own rows. A signal day is not evaluable if, in the 200 sessions to the signal, any session is absent or any Close is missing; if any Volume in the last 20 sessions is missing; or if the benchmark lacks any of the stock's last 64 sessions. A breakout signal (E3, E4) also needs High, Low and Volume for the last 41 sessions, and its absence blocks only the breakout signal. A trade is excluded if the entry session is absent or its Open is missing or not positive; if the exit Open is missing or not positive (a stock that stopped trading before the exit date is recorded as such, not valued at zero); or if the 20-day dollar-volume cost tier cannot be computed (no default tier is assumed). Missing High or Low inside the holding period keeps the trade but removes it from excursion statistics. A volume of exactly 0 is data, not missing. Exclusions are reported by ticker, approach and reason, and the exclusion share of each approach is compared with E1's. A gap above 5 percentage points is flagged as a possible bias | Silent filling or defaulting would bias results in ways that cannot be seen later | exclusions.csv is produced with every run |
| 5 | **E2 paired comparison.** E2 enters at the open after its signal. E1 enters at the open after its signal. Both exit at the open 63 sessions after the E1 entry. Each net return uses its own cost tier on both sides. The E2 leg is held longer, by the number of sessions between the two signals (at most 60), and both holding periods are reported with every pair. This is a supplementary descriptive result, outside the Holm family | Gives a like-for-like exit price while keeping the timing difference visible | Not used for the decision rule |
| 6 | **Population B is descriptive only.** It is used for the missed-winner analysis and never for profitability, thresholds or the decision rule, because it was selected by its outcome. The analysis code raises an error if population B is passed to a strategy statistic | Selection on the outcome biases any return statistic | Enforced in code |
| 7 | **Evidence classes.** An arm needs at least 100 trades from at least 50 distinct tickers in the period (50 trades and 20 tickers for a single exchange). Below that the result is *insufficient sample*, whatever the p-value. Otherwise: *statistically supported improvement* (Holm-adjusted p below 0.05, positive difference); *evidence of worse* (adjusted p below 0.05, negative difference); *evidence of no improvement* (the upper end of a Bonferroni-width interval, 98.33%, is no more than +1.0 percentage point); or *inconclusive*. Insufficient sample is never described as no improvement | Registered text did not say what a small sample means | Used in every comparison table |

**Adjusted-price convention.** All downloads use Yahoo's auto_adjust=True, the same as the scanner. Open, High, Low and Close are back-adjusted for splits and dividends as of the download date. The 50-day and 200-day averages, returns, relative strength and breakout levels are computed on these adjusted prices, as the scanner does. Simulated trade returns are ratios of adjusted opens, so they are split-neutral and include dividends as if reinvested on the ex-date. Cash dividends, taxes and withholding are not modelled separately. Mergers and delistings are not modelled, because the frame holds survivors only. Dollar-volume cost tiers and the secondary price filter (at least $5) use adjusted price times volume. Adjusted prices are lower than the prices visible at the time for stocks that later paid dividends, so older tiers are slightly understated and costs slightly overstated. Raw unadjusted prices are not downloaded.

**Housekeeping, no rule change:** frame.csv gains an `excluded_from_sample` column and download_log.csv gains `in_sample`, so a ticker that is both a discovery and in population C is identifiable.

**Tests after these changes:** exporter and validator 31 passed; replay, execution and statistics 60 passed. 20 deliberate breakages of the new rules were each caught.
